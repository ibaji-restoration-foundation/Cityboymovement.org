About City Boy Movement

City Boy Movement – Ibaji Branch is a local organizational platform serving as a point of connection, communication, coordination and community engagement within Ibaji Local Government Area, Kogi State.

Who We Are

City Boy Movement – Ibaji Branch brings together people who participate in the movement's officially announced programmes, activities, meetings and community engagements.

The branch website provides a central place where visitors can learn about the movement, view leadership information, follow activities, read published news and find information about upcoming programmes.

Our Purpose

The purpose of the branch platform is to strengthen communication, organization and participation among people connected with the movement in Ibaji.

Through this platform, official information about meetings, activities, programmes and community engagement can be shared with members and interested visitors.
